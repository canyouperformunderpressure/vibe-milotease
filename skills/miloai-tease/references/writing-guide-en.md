# Writing Guide

Target style: **high-control, highly interactive, de-clichéd, direct, and crisp adult writing.**
This guide serves as the universal standard for Milo tease creation and **applies to all authored text across the project**: including spoken character dialogue, scene narration, sensory and physical action beats, UI choices and button labels, notifications, game mechanism feedback, and intimate/NSFW interactions.

---

## 1. General Style and De-clichéing (Humanizer)

1. **Reject Cheap AI Tropes and Overwrought Purple Prose**
   - Strictly avoid melodramatic adjective stacking and canned web-fiction vocabulary (e.g., cliché "smirks", "smoldering gazes", "ineffable longing", or stock anatomical exaggerations).
   - Prohibit formulaic physical mannerisms (e.g., repeatedly "raising an eyebrow with a mocking curve", "looking down from above", or "cutting off all avenues of escape").
   - Eliminate redundant adverbs and decorative adjectives. Keep descriptions, commands, and spoken lines **direct, crisp, clean, and physically grounded**.

2. **Strict Constraints on Punctuation and Em Dashes**
   - Eliminate em dash (—) and en dash (–) abuse. Do not use dashes to manufacture fake dramatic pauses or synthetic tension.
   - Avoid mechanical "rule of three" phrasing (e.g., runs of three consecutive adjectives or three parallel phrases).
   - Avoid exclamation mark bombardments; impact comes from concrete action and situational stakes, not punctuation.

3. **Strict Rules on Metaphors and Sensory Word-Salad**
   - **No Explanatory Metaphors:** If an action, physical event, or mental state has already been directly described through objective prose, do not append a subjective metaphor to re-explain it.
   - **Restricted Targets:** Never use metaphors to describe a character's **tone, voice, cadence, eyes, gaze, or line of sight** (idiomatic phrasing excepted; strictly avoid purple tropes like "eyes locking on like a hawk").
   - **Prohibit AI Sensory Word-Salad:** Strictly forbid stitching anatomical terms with environmental adjectives into bizarre pseudo-poetic combinations (e.g., awkward constructs like "slits glowing in murky water" or "petals shimmering under dim aquatic glints"). Descriptions must reflect genuine, tactile human sensory reality.

## 2. Prohibition of Prompts, Camera Cues, and System Leakage

1. **Strictly Forbid Characters Reciting AI Image Prompts and Camera Directives**
   - **Severe AI Tell:** Having characters speak image generation prompts, camera angles, or external stage directions aloud as in-character dialogue (e.g., *"Grabbing both cheeks of my ass to look back at you,"* *"Labia fully revealed from the rear camera angle,"* or *"Resting the surfboard on my shoulder to look at you"*).
   - Characters are living participants inside the fiction, not prompt readers for an image model. Character lines must be natural spoken words driven by their immediate emotion and motivation. Posture and visual presentation belong in **the accompanying image and the scene narration**, never voiced by the character as self-narration.

2. **Prohibit Prompt Tags and Meta Brackets in Authored Text**
   - Strictly prohibit prompt metadata, shot notes, and bracketed tags (e.g., `【Full Frontal Nude】`, `【Extreme Close-up】`, `【Phase 1】`) anywhere in player-facing text. Narrative delivery must remain clean and fully immersive.

3. **Prohibit Characters Acting as System Manuals and Rule Spreadsheets**
   - Spoken dialogue must never explain UI elements or engine mechanics (e.g., *"If you want to leave, click the button on the right whenever your willpower allows"*).
   - Game penalties and state evaluations must never be written as raw engine log reports or administrative rulebooks (e.g., *"Unpermitted ejaculation detected; penalized as severe violation (-25 Willpower)"*).
   - Mechanical tension must be expressed through **immediate character reaction (rebuke, cold laughter, punishment)** and **the player's visceral physical experience**, not through pasted log dumps.

## 3. Scene Structure, Pacing, and Entrance Beats

1. **Break the Rigid "One Page = One Dialogue + One Environment" Assembly Line**
   - Prohibit dividing the entire scene into an automated, formulaic pattern (e.g., twelve consecutive pages each consisting solely of `say: dialogue` followed immediately by `say: environment` + timer + jump).
   - Scene presentation requires dynamic rhythm, breathing room, and organic variation:
     - Establish spatial orientation, weather, and distance before jumping into character interaction.
     - Allow consecutive beats of spoken banter, hesitation, or mockery without forcing decorative environmental filler into every transition.
     - Advance visuals and metronome intensity with physical actions, quiet scrutiny, or focused commands when the moment demands it.
     - Consolidate closely linked narrative beats rather than chopping scenes into single-line mechanical fragments.

2. **Scene Entrances & Second-Person Immersion: "You arrive at... You see..." (Reject God's-Eye Captions)**
   - **Grounded Second-Person Perspective:** Ground the narration directly in the player's immediate physical sensory reality (*"You step onto the creaking planks of the abandoned pier, the cool sea spray misting against your face. Ahead in the shallow surf, you see..."*). The player must feel physically present in the space rather than reading a detached art catalog caption.
   - **Prohibit Detached Omniscient Captions:** Strictly forbid disembodied third-person descriptions like *"The red-haired succubus stands facing the sea, caressing her long hair, her bikini accentuating her waist and hips."* Who is observing? From where? Always anchor visual reveals through the player's own gaze and movement.
   - **No Abrupt Barked Commands:** Forbid characters abruptly barking drill-instructor orders upon entrance (e.g., shouting *"Stand straight!"* before any eye contact or situational acknowledgment). The character must react naturally to your footsteps, your gaze, and your approach.
3. **No Omniscient or Meta-Spoilers**
   - Narration must stay strictly grounded in the immediate moment and local sensory information.
   - Strictly prohibit omniscient, predictive phrasing (e.g., "Little did they know...", "This foreshadowed...", "As events would later prove...").

4. **One Line, One Intent**
   - One spoken line should carry one primary conversational intent.
   - Do not force a single sentence to handle physical action broadcasting, exposition, objective delivery, threats, and teasing simultaneously. Split complex exchanges across multiple distinct beats.

## 4. Character Agency and Will Baseline (Emotional Downgrade Mechanism)

1. **Maintain Baseline Rationality and Agency**
   - Characters must never exhibit absolute despair, total mental breakdown, or loss of the will to survive.
   - When facing extreme pressure, superior force, or crisis events, **forcibly downgrade emotional responses to: [Vigilance / Tension / Gritting Teeth / Seeking Counter-Strategies]**.
   - Regardless of situational disadvantage, characters must preserve basic rational judgment and intentional action.

2. **Handle Interiority Based on the Player Role**
   - **Open Self-Insert:** Leave space for the player to supply their own emotions and attachments. Avoid stating unearned subjective conclusions as absolute facts.
   - **Defined-Character Roleplay:** Subjective judgments and hesitation are permitted if consistent with established characterization, but avoid abrupt, unearned declarations of total emotional surrender.

## 5. Spoken Dialogue: React, Don't Explain

1. **Focus on Immediate Interaction and Character Motivation**
   - Dialogue should primarily respond to concrete, recent player actions: choices, hesitation, obedience, defiance, errors, or status shifts (e.g., observing, judging, correcting, rewarding, penalizing, or questioning).
   - All speech must remain firmly grounded in the character's established personality, underlying motives, and immediate situation, rather than bending to mechanically push game rules.
   - Avoid abstract thematic summaries, stage monologues, or forced trailer-like closing slogans.

2. **Composure over Petulant Rage (Authority and Cadence)**
   - Regardless of whether a character is aloof, haughty, dominant, yandere, or gentle, genuine authority and presence stem from firm conviction and psychological composure, not from hysterical screaming, insults, or cheap vulgar abuse.
   - Strictly avoid characters bursting into unprovoked hostile cursing or meaningless barking. Even in conflict or when dispensing punishment, spoken lines should reflect purpose, control, and emotional poise.
   - Maintain natural spoken cadence and dynamic breathing room. Do not peg intensity at maximum on every single line.

3. **Voice Uniqueness and Rejecting Universal Templates**
   - Avoid generic AI stock lines. Sentence length, vocabulary habits, verbal mannerisms, and emotional distance must feel distinct and tailored to each specific character.

## 6. Scene Narration and Action Prose

1. **Sensory Grounding over Empty Decoration**
   - Narration establishes spatial shifts, physical continuity, environmental contrast, and immediate physical sensations (temperature, sea spray, shadow, lighting, muscle tension, breath).
   - Trim narration by **eliminating redundant decoration and filler**, not by **stripping away necessary atmosphere and physical reality**.

2. **Show Physical Interaction over Evaluation**
   - Use concrete physical action and contact to drive the scene rather than offering abstract qualitative evaluations.

## 7. Adult & NSFW Interaction Guidelines

1. **De-instructionalization (No Lab Reports)**
   - Never write erotic or intimate encounters as biology lectures, medical reports, or anatomical balance sheets.
   - Do not itemize erogenous zones, mechanism ratings, or physical reaction tiers in narrative prose.
   - Authentic tension comes from character stakes, physical credibility, pride, teasing, who cracks first, and who gives in, not from anatomical terminology stacking.

2. **Direct, Natural Vocabulary Serving Plot and Character**
   - Use direct, natural, scene-appropriate language; avoid artificial euphemisms or purple substitutes, but do not mechanically spam explicit terms merely to appear explicit.
   - Intimate contact is an extension of character relationships and current situational stakes. It must not degenerate into a detached, mechanical organ-motion log.

3. **Strict Physical Plausibility and Spatial Logic**
   - Posture, distance, sequence of contact, and leverage must conform to physical human anatomy.
   - One orifice cannot simultaneously accommodate multiple objects. Descriptions must never conflict with participants' physical positions or temporal sequences.

## 8. UI and Interactive Text

1. **Integrate Choice and Button Labels Naturally**
   - Choice and button text should reflect the player's immediate intent or reaction (e.g., *"Bite your lip and hold"*, *"Look away"*, *"Release your grip"*), rather than mechanical engine codes.
   - Prompts, status labels, and helper text should remain concise and preserve full immersion.

## 9. Final Review Checklist

Before approving any authored text, verify against this checklist:

- [ ] **Universal Text Scope:** Do dialogue, narration, UI labels, options, notifications, and scene feedback all comply with this guide?
- [ ] **Structure & Pacing:** Has the rigid "one page = one dialogue + one environment" formula been broken? Does the scene opening feel physically grounded rather than like a barked military order?
- [ ] **Motivation & Composure:** Do character lines align with their persona and motives? Is the text free of unprovoked petulant rage, cursing, or screeching?
- [ ] **Sensory Naturalness:** Are AI word-salad constructs (like "slits glowing in murky water") completely eliminated in favor of tangible physical reality?
- [ ] **No Prompts / Camera Cues:** Is the dialogue completely free of characters reciting SD/MJ prompts, camera orientations, or self-narrated posing instructions? Are all `【】` tags removed?
- [ ] **No System Explanations:** Are characters prevented from acting as UI manuals? Are penalties and rules delivered through narrative drama rather than raw system logs?
- [ ] **De-clichéing:** Are there any cheap AI stock phrases, purple romance tropes, or bloated adjective clusters?
- [ ] **Punctuation & Metaphors:** Are em dashes (—/–) cut? Are metaphors kept out of voice and gaze descriptions?
- [ ] **Narrative Perspective:** Does narration stay strictly grounded in the immediate moment without omniscient spoilers?
- [ ] **Agency Baseline:** When under threat, does the character remain rational, active, and downgraded to vigilance/counter-strategy rather than collapse?
- [ ] **Voice Uniqueness:** Does the character sound distinct, idiosyncratic, and alive, rather than like a generic AI tease generator?
