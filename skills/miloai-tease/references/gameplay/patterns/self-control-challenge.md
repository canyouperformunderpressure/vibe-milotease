# Self-control challenge

## Design profile

- **Role:** Anchor or modifier pattern
- **Best for:** restraint, obedience, precision, tension from maintaining a rule
- **Typical scale:** scene / phase
- **Can lead a phase:** Yes
- **Player mainly:** maintains a behavioral constraint until release, success, or self-reported failure
- **Combines well with:** `timed-challenge.md`, `metronome-stroking.md`, `retry-loop.md`, `surrender-option.md`

A self-control challenge asks the player not merely to act, but to maintain a constraint while other pressure continues.

Examples include staying silent, holding a posture, keeping balance, not touching a forbidden area, maintaining a prescribed speed, or continuing without breaking a stated rule.

## Design recipe

1. State one observable constraint clearly.
2. Apply pressure that makes the constraint harder to maintain.
3. Give the player a way to report failure when the system cannot detect it automatically.
4. Decide whether failure causes a retry, consequence, branch, or graceful exit.
5. Escalate by duration, distraction, or stricter precision rather than by stacking many unrelated rules.
