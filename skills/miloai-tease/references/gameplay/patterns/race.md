# Race / target-under-deadline challenge

## Design profile

- **Role:** Anchor scene pattern
- **Best for:** urgency, competition, measurable performance, success/failure contrast
- **Typical scale:** scene / short phase
- **Can lead a phase:** Yes
- **Player mainly:** reaches a concrete target before a timer or opposing pace wins
- **Combines well with:** `timed-challenge.md`, `metronome-stroking.md`, `retry-loop.md`, `progression.md`

A race gives the player a measurable target and a deadline. Unlike a generic timed challenge, success is explicitly about beating a target rather than merely enduring until time expires.

## Design recipe

1. Give a concrete target the player can understand immediately.
2. Start a visible or hidden deadline depending on the intended pressure.
3. Route success and timeout to meaningfully different feedback.
4. Optionally offer a rematch with a changed target, pace, or consequence.
5. Avoid impossible arithmetic: target and time should imply a physically plausible required pace for the chosen difficulty.
