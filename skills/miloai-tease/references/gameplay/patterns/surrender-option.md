# Persistent surrender option

## Design profile

- **Role:** Cross-phase modifier pattern
- **Best for:** pressure with agency, temptation to quit, consequence-based risk decisions
- **Typical scale:** scene / cross-phase
- **Can lead a phase:** No
- **Player mainly:** chooses whether to continue under pressure or trigger a known/unknown surrender consequence
- **Combines well with:** `timed-challenge.md`, `self-control-challenge.md`, `edge-cum-permission.md`, `metronome-stroking.md`

A persistent surrender option remains available while another challenge runs. It is not simply an exit button: choosing it is an authored gameplay decision with consequences.

## Design recipe

- Make the action easy to reach when pressure is high.
- Make its consequence legible enough that the choice feels intentional, while allowing uncertainty when that serves the scene.
- Remove or replace the control when the surrounding phase changes.
- Never leave stale persistent controls active after their target branch is no longer valid.
