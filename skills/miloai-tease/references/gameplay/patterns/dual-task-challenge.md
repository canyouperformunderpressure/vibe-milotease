# Dual-task challenge

## Design profile

- **Role:** Anchor or modifier pattern
- **Best for:** divided attention, cognitive pressure, mistakes under arousal or distraction
- **Typical scale:** scene / short phase
- **Can lead a phase:** Yes
- **Player mainly:** performs one ongoing action while simultaneously counting, calculating, observing, remembering, or answering
- **Combines well with:** `metronome-stroking.md`, `timed-challenge.md`, `retry-loop.md`

A dual-task challenge deliberately makes the player manage two demands at once. The second task should be simple in isolation; the difficulty comes from doing it while the primary task continues.

## Design recipe

1. Establish one primary action that must continue.
2. Add one secondary attention task: counting, arithmetic, visual counting, memory, choice, or timed response.
3. Define what counts as a mistake or timeout.
4. Give immediate feedback or a consequence, then either continue or retry with a variation.
5. Escalate by changing only one dimension at a time: faster primary action, harder question, shorter answer window, or an additional rule.

Do not make the cognitive task so complex that the player stops engaging with the primary interaction entirely.

## Example forms

- Keep a beat while counting to a target.
- Count visible objects, then answer under a short deadline.
- Remember a sequence while following changing pacing instructions.
- Answer a simple question without stopping the current action.
